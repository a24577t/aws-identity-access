# Methodology Observations

Immutable, append-only register of observations of the methodology in practice (see
[README.md](README.md)). Each entry carries a stable `MO-NNN` identifier, states
verifiable evidence and its observed impact only — no disposition and no remedy — and
is never edited after it is appended. Dispositions live solely in the review records
under [`reviews/`](reviews/) (`MVR-NNN`); an observation no review references is
unadjudicated.

## Entry format

```markdown
### MO-NNN — <short title>

- **Recorded:** <YYYY-MM-DD>, <participant>
- **Context:** <transition, skill step, or gate where observed>
- **Evidence:** <verifiable repository evidence: commits, PRs, artifacts, diffs>
- **Observed impact:** <effect on the methodology in practice>
```
